I wanted to see whether the bot team could build a game in the spirit of Sid Meier's Pirates! from scratch. It has our own name, art, music and code. The result is **Salt and Glory**, a 2.5D pirate adventure set in the Caribbean around 1660.

**[Play Salt and Glory](https://mikenev.github.io/salt-and-glory/)** · [Source on GitHub](https://github.com/mikenev/salt-and-glory)

## A career at sea

You start as a young captain with a small sloop and a little gold. From there it's up to you. You can trade sugar and tobacco between 12 real ports held by England, Spain, France and the Dutch Republic. You can take a letter of marque from a governor and hunt their enemies legally. Or you can just turn pirate and let everyone hate you. Each nation remembers what you've done, and every deed adds to your fame. When you're ready, retire from the captain's log and see your final score.

## Broadsides and boarding

Out at sea, the wind matters. Running with it is fast, and sailing into it barely moves you. Line up a broadside and pick your shot. Round shot smashes the hull, chain shot tears the sails, and grape shot thins the crew. Get alongside and you can board her. Boarding switches to a side-on sword duel on the deck. A high attack beats a low guard, a low attack beats a high guard, and a well-timed block parries. Drive the other captain overboard and her ship and cargo are yours. There are five ship classes to capture or buy: sloop, brigantine, fluyt, frigate and galleon.

## Buried treasure

Taverns and captured ships turn up pieces of treasure maps. Collect all four, and the map shows the real stretch of coast where the X is. Sail there and dig.

## How it was built

A team of AI bots built the game. I directed it and gave notes at each milestone. **Lead Developer Bot** planned the work and merged it. **Game Dev Bot** wrote the code in Unity, and **Art Dev** painted the ships, towns, map and UI. **Audio Dev** wrote four original shanty-style loops and the sound effects. **QA Bot** tested every milestone and played a full career before launch.

Hoist the sails and see how much glory you can win.
