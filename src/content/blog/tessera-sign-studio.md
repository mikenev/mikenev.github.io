The bot team usually makes games, but this time I wanted a tool. I love old mosaic tile signs, the kind with a name spelled out in little square tiles and a checker border around it, so I asked for a page that would plan one for me. The result is **Tessera Sign Studio**.

**[Try Tessera Sign Studio](https://mikenev.github.io/tessera-sign-studio/)** · [Source on GitHub](https://github.com/mikenev/tessera-sign-studio)

## Type, pick, build

Type up to 15 letters across two lines. The second line is set smaller, so it works well for a street number or a subtitle. Pick one of three original fonts (Block, Heavy and Mini) and one of four original styles:

- **Bathhouse**: a white field, blue lettering and a navy and white checker border.
- **Terracotta Station**: warm earth tones with a key pattern border.
- **Seaglass**: soft greens and blues.
- **Midnight Platform**: light letters on a dark field.

The sign updates as you type, tile by tile.

## A real template

Everything is drawn at true size, so you can actually build it:

- **Tile mode** uses 1-inch tiles with a grout gap you can set.
- **Bead mode** uses 5 mm perler beads and splits the design across 29x29 pegboards.
- Colors come from a general set with plain names, and a bill of materials counts how many pieces of each color you need.
- Export an **SVG**, a **PNG** or a printable multi-page **PDF**, with an optional grid overlay with row and column numbers, plus a 1:1 ruler so you can check the scale after printing.

Your last design is remembered, and the page works on phones too. Fair warning: real tile signs are big. A two-line sign can easily be four or five feet wide.

A team of AI bots built it. I directed it and made the design calls.

- **Lead Developer Bot** planned the milestones and merged each piece.
- **Game Dev Bot** wrote the code, fonts included.
- **QA Bot** tested every milestone, down to checking the tile size in the PDF.
