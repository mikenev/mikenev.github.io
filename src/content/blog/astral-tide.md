Most of the games the bot team has made are about going fast or shooting things. This time I wanted something calm. The result is **Astral Tide**, a gentle voyage across a sea of stars where you can't fail.

**[Play Astral Tide](https://mikenev.github.io/astral-tide/)** · [Source on GitHub](https://github.com/mikenev/astral-tide)

## Sail toward the light

You sail a small skiff toward a glowing landmark on the horizon, one leg at a time. A star compass at the top of the screen shows the way, and a soft chime plays when you're on course. Drift too far and the fog and a gentle pull bring you back. Bumps only slow you down. Sail through sparkling star gusts for a burst of speed, and find the star fragments hidden along each leg to light up that leg's constellation in the sky.

## Six legs

The voyage has six legs, and each one ends at its own landmark:

- **Dusk Shallows**: lavender twilight and a gentle swell, sailing toward the Lantern Spire.
- **Aurora Reach**: a teal night under aurora curtains, with glowing currents that carry you along.
- **Ember Drift**: deep indigo skies, shooting stars and drifting meteors.
- **Frost Mirror**: perfectly still silver water, faceted ice floes and whales that swim and breach out past the floes. Chain the star gusts together for a longer boost, and look for the gold sparkles that mark the star fragments.
- **The Cosmic Sea**: a purple nebula and big rolling waves. Thread the star buoys on the way to the White Star.
- **Nebula Run**: the finale. Twice the speed, twice the distance and twice the stars, riding the Cosmic Sea's swell.

All six legs are open from the start, and your progress and best times are saved.

## How it was built

A team of AI bots built the game. I directed it and gave notes at each milestone.

- **Lead Developer Bot** planned the milestones, handed out the work and merged each piece.
- **Game Dev Bot** built the game in Unity.
- **Art Dev** made the original art, from the skiff and the skies to the ice and the whales.
- **Audio Dev** made the original music and sound effects.
- **QA Bot** tested every milestone.

Raise the sail, follow the stars and enjoy the quiet.
